Macro values are filtered on vintage_date, never observation_date: a CPI print is stamped with the month it measures and published about six weeks later, then revised. Filtering on the observation date would show a backtest numbers that did not exist yet.

The three perpetual panels share one x-axis and have separate y-axes on purpose. Price, a funding rate near zero and an open interest in the tens of thousands share no scale, and putting two of them on one plot with two y-scales would let the author choose the correlation you see.
